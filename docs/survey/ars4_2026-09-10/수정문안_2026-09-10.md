### 전문 (193단어)

> This study designed and developed EASWA (Exploring All-Sky Web App), an educational web
> platform that reorganizes public astronomical data for school astronomy inquiry. A
> walkthrough analysis of four public data services found that school use needs added
> support in connecting professional data structures to inquiry topics, preparing the data
> for analysis, and interpreting results. With theoretical, curriculum, and textbook review,
> five design principles were articulated and implemented as a seven-step inquiry flow
> across three modules: TESS transits, KMTNet microlensing, and Gaia cluster
> color-magnitude diagrams. The transit module's analysis was checked against published
> values; the platform's radius ratio ran about 12% low. Two formative reviews were
> conducted with 13 mainly in-service science teachers and 13 pre-service Earth science
> teachers. Items on reduced technical execution load scored high in both rounds, while
> items on the reference-comparison screen scored lowest among the response items. Requests
> for further support with terminology and graph interpretation, and for classroom
> worksheets and teacher guides, appeared in both reviews. Because the cohorts and platform
> versions differed together, score differences were not interpreted as effects of the
> revision. The study presents technical-execution and data-interpretation support as
> distinct design tasks.

## 2. 그림 캡션 — 화면 글자를 읽어야 하는 주장을 뺀다

그림 속 글자가 종이에서 1.8~2.6 pt다. 학회지 최소 활자는 8.4 pt다. **그림은 화면 구성을
보이는 것이고 글자를 읽히려는 것이 아니므로**, 캡션이 화면 안 글자를 근거로 삼는 자리를
없앤다.

### 2-1. 그림 4 캡션

지금 캡션이 「자료 출처와 관측 정보를 함께 제시한다」처럼 화면 글자를 읽어야 확인되는
주장을 한다. **읽어야 하는 값은 본문에 적고 캡션은 무엇이 보이는 화면인지만 말한다.**

### 2-2. 그림 4(b) — 재촬영으로 해결됨

「한 픽셀에 여러 별이 들어갈 수 있음을 보이고」가 빈 격자를 가리키고 있었다.
